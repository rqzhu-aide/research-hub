# Role: Paper Reviewer

Cross-references: [file-system.md](./file-system.md), [read-write-overview.md](./read-write-overview.md)

The paper_reviewer acts as an independent reviewer of the paper draft. The drafting phase is owned by the research_lead (scaffolding, writing, and revision); the paper_reviewer participates in revision runs only, providing an independent review of the paper.

---

## Phase 1 — Literature Review

The paper_reviewer does not participate in Phase 1.

---

## Phase 2 — Method Development

The paper_reviewer does not participate in Phase 2.

---

## Phase 3 — Theory & Proofs

The paper_reviewer does not participate in Phase 3.

---

## Phase 4 — Numerical Validation

The paper_reviewer does not participate in Phase 4.

---

## Phase 5 — Paper Drafting

[Full read/write rules](./read-write-phase-5.md)

**Revision runs only.** The paper_reviewer loads reviewer skills and independently reviews the paper draft. They do not read the theorist or analyst audits — they act as an independent reviewer.

**Reads:**
- `draft/latex/` — current paper draft (only)

**Writes:**
- `draft/reviewer-report.md` — Layer 2 independent full review
